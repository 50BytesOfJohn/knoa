The top bar, one decision at a time

Four questions about the strip above the cards: where view and display controls go, what happens to the kind chips, what suggestions show, and how applied filters stay visible. Each has three or four options side by side, then one clickable prototype of the mix I recommend.

Builds on round 1. docs/design/search-explorations.html (Nov 20) chose "Trail": ↵ locks a word in as a token, a row under the pill offers the next word. This round keeps that engine and settles the parts round 1 left open. One correction to the brief: the button inside the pill today is View options (summaries, Up next strip), not Settings. Settings already lives in the sidebar footer (gear, ⌘,), so the real question is where view controls go.

Research

What makes a search bar feel familiar

People bring habits from Finder, Photos, Mail, Gmail and Spotlight. Matching those habits is what makes a new app feel easy on day one. These are the patterns that matter for Knoa.

View controls live at the top right

On macOS the trailing edge of the toolbar holds the controls that must always be reachable: view switchers, inspectors, the More menu. Finder, Photos, Mail and Notes all put "how it looks" there, away from "what it shows".

Apple HIG: Toolbars
Keep "what" apart from "how"

Linear and Notion keep filters with the search and put layout, sort and density in a separate Display menu. Mixing them inside the search field, as Knoa does now, makes the field look like a settings bar.

Linear, Notion (product review)
Suggestions teach the vocabulary

Autocomplete is most useful when it shows people what they can search by (kinds, tags, sites) rather than just finishing words. Keep it short (around 8–10 rows on desktop), plain, and set the predicted part apart from what was typed.

Baymard: autocomplete design
Applied filters must be visible and removable

Without an overview of what's applied, people can't tell why results are missing. Baymard found 28% of large sites still lack one. Each filter needs its own × and there should be a way to clear them all.

Baymard: applied filters
Refinements appear after you search

Gmail shows its filter chips (has attachment, from, date) only once you've searched, right under the field. It's a well-known pattern: no clutter at rest, a nudge right when you need to narrow.

Google Workspace: search chips
Browsing still beats searching

People reach saved things by navigating 56–68% of the time and search mostly when they can't remember where something is (Knoa's own research). So one-click kind filters at rest are worth keeping in some form, not only behind typing.

Question 1

Where do view and display controls go?

Today the sliders button sits inside the pill. You also want to add a view switch (masonry, grid, list) later. Neither belongs in the search field.

Recommendation: 1B, a corner capsule.

A small floating capsule at the top right of the content: the layout switch and a Display button. It sits level with the pill, moves left when the detail panel opens, and is where future view controls go (zoom, group by). Settings stays where it is in the sidebar footer, so there's one gear, not two. If the corner ever gets crowded, fold it into 1C.

Question 2

What happens to the kind chips?

The row of kinds under the pill is the only way to filter without typing. It's also always on screen and can reach two lines in a varied library.

Recommendation: 2D, one adaptive row.

Keep a single, quieter row, because one-click browsing by kind is what people do most. At rest it lists kinds with counts. When you search it changes to whatever splits the current results: kinds first if they're mixed, then tags. Picking one moves it into the pill as a token and it leaves the row, so the "All" chip is no longer needed: removing the token is "All". Cap it at one line; extras go behind "More".

Question 3

What do suggestions show, and how?

The dropdown is where people learn what Knoa can search by. It has two jobs: before typing, get you back to where you were; while typing, turn words into precise filters or open the bookmark straight away.

Recommendation: 3A while typing, 3B on an empty focus.

One list attached to the pill, at most about 10 rows. The first row is always "Search for …" so ↵ does what you expect. Then filters grouped by type (Kinds, Folders, Tags, Sites, Fields), each with a count and never at zero. Then up to three bookmarks, so ↵ on one opens it. The typed part is plain and the completion is bold, as Baymard recommends. Keyboard hints at the bottom teach ↵ ⇥ ⌫ without a help page.

Question 4

How are applied filters shown?

Filters must be visible and removable. The choice is whether they live inside the search field or in a separate bar.

Recommendation: 4A, tokens in the pill, with 4C's overflow.

The pill is the single source of truth: what you see in it is the query, the same as Finder and Mail. Each token has an ×, ⌫ on an empty field selects and then removes the last one, and a click opens it to change the value. When the tokens would crowd out the text, the oldest collapse into a "+2" token. "Clear" and "Save as folder" appear at the end of the refine row only while something is applied.

Still open

Layout switch: icons or menu?

Three icons are faster and familiar from Finder; a "Masonry ▾" menu is quieter. I chose icons because you expect more layouts to come and the count stays small.

Should Add link move to the corner?

Round 1 suggested putting it beside the view controls. It's an action, not a view setting, so I left it in the sidebar titlebar. Easy to revisit.

Refine row in Gallery and Events

Gallery holds media kinds only, so the row should start with tags there, and Events could offer dates (This week, This month).

Recent searches: kept where?

Per workspace, on this Mac only, last 5. Needs a small store and a "Clear recent" item.
